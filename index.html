About me

Hello,
I'm Lisanne.

A generalist product designer in Berlin with a research & experimentation spine. Five years across consumer trust, B2B logistics and analytics-heavy operations, usually where marketing meets product. Outside of work, mostly upside down: , half marathons, and Yoshi the mini poodle.

01 · Origin

I started with questions, not pixels.

A Sociology MA in Göttingen and a parallel Ethnography and Modern China BA, both finished with distinction in 2020. Six scholarships, three semesters between Singapore, Fudan and Peking University. The questions I loved asking turned out to be exactly the ones good design answers.

02 · Shipping

Then I learned to ship.

Two years at TikTok as a Quality Analyst: weekly UX-quality reviews, cross-functional with User Growth, and an eLearning program on EdCast for 250+ colleagues. Special Contribution Award, Q1 2021. Observation got disciplined, design got tactile.

03 · Spine

Research & growth became my spine.

Solo design lead at Cargoboard, then UX Designer (Growth) at Trusted Shops. The first focus group in a 25-year-old company. Heatmaps that reshaped a brand. A price set on intuition turned into a +15% revenue lift, and 3/3 payment-form tests won at +20% each.

04 · Now

Still curious, just deliberate about it.

Senior Product Designer at Trusted Shops. Leading the IxDF Berlin design community, teaching aerial yoga, training for half marathons, and reading more behavioral science than is strictly healthy. Most of it with Yoshi, my mini poodle, somewhere in the room.

./about.md

Curious by training,
deliberate by habit.

I didn't start in design. I started in research. A Sociology MA in Göttingen and a parallel Ethnography and Modern China BA, both finished with distinction in 2020, plus six scholarships that put me through the National University of Singapore, Fudan, and Peking University. The questions I loved asking (why people do what they do, what makes a product feel effortless) turned out to be exactly what good design answers.

Five years in, I've designed end-to-end across consumer trust, B2B logistics, and analytics-heavy operations, often at the intersection of marketing and product. My spine is research and experimentation: I've run the first focus group in a 25-year-old company, introduced heatmaps that reshaped a brand, and turned a price set on intuition into a 15% revenue lift.

But the label 'growth designer' undersells it. The work that excites me is the full loop: from a fuzzy strategic question to shipped UI to measured outcome, with a design system and a research habit left behind afterward.

Off-hours I teach , lead the IxDF Berlin design community, train for half marathons, draw weekly, and read a lot of behavioral science. Usually with Yoshi, my mini poodle, somewhere nearby.
