The full record.

Curious
by training,
deliberate by habit.

I didn't start in design. I started in research. A Sociology MA in Göttingen and a parallel Ethnography and Modern China BA, both finished with distinction in 2020, plus six scholarships that put me through the National University of Singapore, Fudan, and Peking University. The questions I loved asking (why people do what they do, what makes a product feel effortless) turned out to be exactly what good design answers.

Five years in, I've designed end-to-end across consumer trust, B2B logistics, and analytics-heavy operations, often at the intersection of marketing and product. I'm a generalist by choice, with a T-shaped spine in research and growth. I've run the first focus group in a 25-year-old company, introduced heatmaps that reshaped a brand, and turned a price set on intuition into a 15% revenue lift.

Breadth is the point. The work that excites me is the full loop: from a fuzzy strategic question to shipped UI to measured outcome, with a design system, a research habit, and a sharper team left behind afterward.

Outside of product work I teach , lead the IxDF Berlin design community, train for half marathons, and read more behavioral science than is strictly healthy. Most of it with Yoshi, my mini poodle, somewhere in the room.

Server logs (GitHub Pages)

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you open it, your browser transmits technically necessary data: IP address, date and time, requested URL, referrer, browser, and operating system. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in stable operation). Details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

YouTube embeds

Some case studies embed videos from YouTube (Google Ireland Ltd.). I use the privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), which means YouTube does not set cookies until you press play. Once you start a video, your IP address and interaction data are transmitted to YouTube and Google. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent through clicking play) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Details: Google Privacy Policy.

Local storage

The browser's localStorage remembers your design preferences (variation, accent colour, type scale). This data stays on your device, is never transmitted, and can be cleared from your browser settings at any time.

Getting in touch

If you email me, I process the information you send in order to respond to your enquiry. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. Emails are deleted once the purpose no longer applies.

Your rights
