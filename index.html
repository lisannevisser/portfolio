Hello,
I'm Lisanne.

An Economic and Intercultural Sociologist by education, I found my way into product design, where I focus on data-driven design solutions that combine user-centricity with business performance.

01 · Origin

I started in research

During my master's in Sociology, I decided to also do a second bachelor's in Ethnography and Modern China studies. My focus on Economic Sociology in China created an urge to combine perspectives from all three disciplines. In 2020, I graduated from my master's and my additional bachelor's simultaneously, both with distinction. My interdisciplinary studies led me to do research in China, and several scholarships from the German and Chinese governments let me study at Asia's top universities, namely the National University of Singapore, Fudan University in Shanghai, and Beijing University.

02 · Practice

Where product meets business

My work as a product designer often takes place at the intersection between product and marketing teams, enabling me to help brands navigate between user needs and business KPIs. Thanks to my training in social research, I bring strong qualitative and quantitative user research to the table. Through my professional journey, I've partnered with top brands such as TikTok, Canva, and more. I thrive in collaborative environments defined by curiosity, empathy, and radical candor, which I believe are crucial factors to successful digital solutions with happy teams behind them.

03 · Off-screen

In my free time

In my spare time, I love spending time with friends just as much as I enjoy reading, drawing, and practicing yoga. I love Rocket Yoga and Ashtanga, and I have a 200h yoga teacher training.

In 2024, I did a teacher training in Aerial Yoga. Over the past two years I've been teaching Aerial Yoga classes on the weekends. My own aerial practice slowly shifted from yoga towards acrobatics. Today, I'm at the aerial studio several times a week to practice aerial hammock, aerial hoop or silks.

What I don't
know about you.

This site is a portfolio, not a product. No tracking, no analytics, no ads. Below is an honest list of what still happens technically when you visit.

Controller

Lisanne Visser, George-Stephenson-Str. 2, 10557 Berlin, Germany. Email: mail@lisannevisser.com. Full details in the imprint.

Server logs (GitHub Pages)

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you open it, your browser transmits technically necessary data: IP address, date and time, requested URL, referrer, browser, and operating system. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in stable operation). Details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

YouTube embeds

Some case studies embed videos from YouTube (Google Ireland Ltd.). I use the privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), which means YouTube does not set cookies until you press play. Once you start a video, your IP address and interaction data are transmitted to YouTube and Google. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent through clicking play) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Details: Google Privacy Policy.

Canva and Behance embeds

The Visuals page shows some pieces inline through embeds from Canva (Canva Pty Ltd, Australia) and Behance (Adobe Inc., USA). Nothing is requested while you browse the grid: an embed is only loaded once you open the card it belongs to, and it is removed again when you close it. From the moment it loads, your IP address and browser data go to that provider, and it may set cookies. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent through opening the card) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Details: Canva Privacy Policy, Adobe Privacy Policy.

Fonts

Every typeface on this site is served from this site. There is no Google Fonts request and no font CDN, so loading a page does not hand your IP address to a font provider. The typefaces are credited in the imprint.

Local storage

The browser's localStorage remembers your design preferences (accent colour, type scale). This data stays on your device, is never transmitted, and can be cleared from your browser settings at any time.

Getting in touch

If you email me, I process the information you send in order to respond to your enquiry. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. Emails are deleted once the purpose no longer applies.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, and objection at any time (Art. 15 - 21 GDPR). A short email is enough. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority (for Berlin: Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).
